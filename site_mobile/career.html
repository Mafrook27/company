Designation: SEO Specialist

No of Positions: 1

Experience: 2–3 Years

Role Type: Full-Time

We are looking for an SEO Specialist with 2–3 years of proven experience. You will be responsible for improving organic visibility, driving qualified traffic, and ensuring our content meets the highest standards of E-E-A-T within the financial domain.

Read More

Key Responsibilities

  • Conduct keyword research and optimize finance-related webpages and blogs.
  • Implement on-page SEO: meta tags, content optimization, internal linking, and SERP-focused improvements.
  • Perform regular technical SEO audits and fix issues related to crawling, indexing, page speed, and Core Web Vitals.
  • Apply finance-relevant schema markup (FAQ, How-To, Organization, Financial Service).
  • Build high-quality backlinks through outreach and guest posting.
  • Collaborate with content writers to create SEO-friendly finance content.
  • Monitor keyword rankings, traffic, and conversions; generate SEO performance reports.

Required Skills

  • 2–3 years of hands-on SEO experience.
  • Solid understanding of E-E-A-T and YMYL SEO standards.
  • Expertise in keyword research, SERP analysis, and competitive finance niche research.
  • Strong analytical skills with proficiency in GA4, GSC, and SEO tools.
  • Basic understanding of technical SEO concepts.
  • Excellent communication and documentation abilities.
  • Experience working on finance or loan-related websites is a plus, but not mandatory.
