Microsoft Practice

Statlight has been a recognized Microsoft Global Partner since inception, offering solutions, services and implementations based on Microsoft Platform. With strong team of Microsoft certified resources, we bring deep industry expertise and cross-vertical experience to your business challenges.

Open Source

Statlight has vast experience in software development and large scale open source web development for our global clients hailing from diverse industry segments. We have successfully developed some of the most reliable, secure, high performance and dynamic web applications using various open source platforms.
