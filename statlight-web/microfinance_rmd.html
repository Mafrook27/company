Micro Finance

It has always been a need in the industry to deliver services to meet the need of lenders, providers and industry dynamics. Statlight understands the ever changing and demanding features and we flexibly map our service delivery to your evolving needs.

At Statlight, we help define your requirements, write specifications and design, develop, test and integrate software across multiple platforms, enabling your systems to function in new operating environments.

We have a wealth of experience in working with major lenders and service providers in the industry.

Our Portfolio of services include,

  • Servicing platforms for Microfinance and Lending Industry
  • Consumer financial services software for payday loans, instalment loans, car title loans and cash advance
  • Lead Capture and Loan Origination Systems (LOS) including Lead Capture, Loan Applications, Underwriting, Credit pull, Decisioning, Backend reporting and Web portal
  • Build and Integrate Landing Pages
  • Loan Management Systems (LMS) software for Loan Origination, Underwriting, Amortization, Processing, and Tracking
  • Decision Support System (DSS) applications and rules-based engines for streamlining loan pipeline processes
  • Support for multiple loan types including fixed, variable, CSO-CAB model and credit lines
  • Secure self-service customer portals including document uploading, viewing statements, payment processing, payoff calculations, balances, and more
  • Lead Management and CRM Development and Integration
  • Long term maintenance and support for your existing platforms on flexible engagement models
  • Modernization and Re-engineering of your existing software platforms
  • Develop your own in-house Loan Management System
  • Architecture redefining and Optimization of desktop and client-server application to pure web, Saas and cloud
  • Mobile and Cloud enablement of your existing systems
  • Cooperative development, in which our experts work with your in-house IT personnel to jointly analyze, design, implement, test and integrate new systems.
