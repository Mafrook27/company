Profile

Statlight is a software engineering services provider headquartered in Bangalore, India. Statlight was established in 2011 by seasoned professionals who have expertise and experience to create optimal value and enable compelling end-user applications and services. Though we are growing rapidly, we still operate like a startup, innovating constantly and always looking for ways to improve.

We are serving a diverse range of enterprise customers by providing innovative solutions to help them stay ahead in their IT needs. Our inherent management and domain strengths backed by small, strong and responsive teams make Statlight as a well established and fast growing business. Inherent means, Statlight's highly skilled and experienced pool of innovative, aggressive and smart working professionals run Statlight - A company run by its people.

What’s great is that we do it all under the one roof to produce a cohesive end result every time.

Our key service offerings include Cloud ready application development, Enterprise Web Application development, E-Business solutions, Client-Server Application Development, Systems Integration, Application Software, Strategic Consulting and development of comprehensive, end-to-end software solutions.

With strong partners and collaboration with Universities, we have access to specialized skills anywhere in the world, to solve any problem, through our engagement model. Enhanced project collaboration tools and our well-codified, company-wide processes assure consistent, well-planned, measurable and reliable delivery.
